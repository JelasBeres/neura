How it works

Real market data in, a portfolio out. In between, a model of a living thing.

No neural network trained on prices. Each organism is a published model from biology, run as math, fed the same numbers every hour.

01 · FOOD

The market becomes food

Every hour each of the 11 assets gets one number: price momentum, mean reversion, on-chain buying on Robinhood Chain, or news tone. That number is what the organism smells.

02 · BRAIN

The organism reacts

A slime mold grows tubes, a worm's 302 neurons spike, fish cells fire, ants lay pheromone, bees dance, bacteria tumble. Same food, six very different mechanisms.

03 · PORTFOLIO

Its state is a portfolio

Where the organism ends up becomes its share of capital in each asset. Every trade pays 0.3%. The result is scored against simply holding all 11 equally.

Walk-forward, 4 folds

Each organism is born fresh at the start of a fold, picks its setting only from data before it, then trades the fold blind.

48 settings, 5 seeds

The same tuning budget for every organism. Each test runs with five random seeds and averages them, so luck washes out.

0.3% per trade

Costs are paid on everything traded. Hold trades once; an organism that churns pays for it.

Blind twin

The same organism with no food runs alongside. If it does as well, the food isn't doing anything.

Four gates

Beat hold on return and Sharpe, no deeper drawdown, and win at least 3 of 4 folds. All four, or it doesn't count.

Sealed holdout

The last three months stay unopened until an experiment passes every gate. It is opened once.

What this is not

Honest about the limits.

  • Not living organisms: mathematical models of how real ones behave, taken from published research.
  • No proven edge yet. No experiment has passed all four gates, so the holdout is still sealed.
  • Paper trading only. No real money is traded and nothing here is financial advice.
  • Past test results, even honest ones, don't promise future returns.
